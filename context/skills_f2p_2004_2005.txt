F2P SKILL TRAINING — 2004-2005 ERA (RS2, pre-market)
================================================
Project context: LostCity revision 274 (2004). 15 F2P skills: Attack,
Defence, Strength, Hitpoints, Ranged, Prayer, Magic, Cooking,
Woodcutting, Fishing, Firemaking, Crafting, Smithing,
Mining, Runecraft (Fletching training is members-only, see below). No market yet (our homemade exchange, to be implemented
later). Methods only, no hourly
rates, no prices.

GENERAL
-------
- Combat XP: 4 XP per damage in the trained stance (Attack, Strength or
  Defence), shared XP in Controlled stance, plus Hitpoints XP per damage
  on every hit. Scimitar is the
  fastest training weapon (Tip.It: tested better than battleaxe).
- Gear rule (Tip.It): best armour for your Defence (iron 1, steel 5,
  black 10, mithril 20, addy 30, rune 40), best scimitar for your
  Attack, strength amulet always. No trimmed/dhide for show (same
  bonuses, wasted money), no wizard robes for melee.
- Pures: green d'hide chaps/vambs at 40+ Ranged with 1 Defence.
- Free food: monks at the Edgeville monastery heal you by talking to
  them; fishing your own food (salmon or better); lobsters fished on
  Karamja for the volcano trip.

MELEE (Attack / Strength / Defence / Hitpoints)
------------------------------------------------
Low levels / pures:
- Cows (Lumbridge): start here, keep cowhides for cash.
- Monks (Edgeville monastery): free healing by talking, no food needed.
- Hill giants (Edgeville Dungeon, brass key, stairs room): big bones +
  limpwurt roots income on the side, bury bones for Prayer XP, keep
  runes. Rangers compete for spawns.
Higher levels:
- Gear: rune full helm, strength amulet, rune platebody/legs, rune
  kiteshield, green d'hide vambraces (at 40 Ranged), rune scimitar,
  team cape. Only the scimmy for training.
- Lesser demons (82, Karamja volcano: Port Sarim boat 30 gp, west to the
  volcano): good alchable drops (rune meds). Bring alch runes (natures;
  fires drop there). Lobsters fished on site, or noted supplies +
  general-store "bank" trick.
- Moss giants (level 42, Wilderness): big bones, solid mid-level XP.
  Bank at Edgeville.
- Ice warriors/giants: listed for higher levels.
- Goblins (level 2, across the Lumbridge bridge): levels 1-10.

RANGED
------
- Gear: best bow + arrows you can use; 40 Ranged unlocks green d'hide
  vambraces (also for melee pures). Ranged armour is cheap (crafted or
  dropped).
- Low: chickens (feathers for Fishing bait money), cows, hill giants
  (safe spots).
- High: moss giants (safe spots), lessers (Karamja).
- Safe-spotting keeps food use down.

MAGIC (standard spellbook, same in 2004)
-----------------------------------------
- Levels: Wind Strike 1, confusion/weakening curses, Wind Bolt 17, Curse
  19, Bind 20, Low Alch 21, teleports (Varrock 25, Lumbridge 31, Falador
  37), Superheat 43, High Alch 55.
- Training path: strike/bolt spells on monsters (hill giants, monks,
  lessers) -> Curse for cheap XP with body runes -> Low/High
  Alchemy on drops (rune meds from lessers) -> teleports for utility XP.
- 33 Magic unlocks wine telegrab (money method, see money doc).
- Cheap route: splashing Curses; expensive route: alching + teleporting.
- Amulet of magic for accuracy; wizard robes.

PRAYER
------
- Bury bones where you train: regular bones, big bones (hill/moss/ice
  giants). No F2P altars in 2004.
- Prayer gear: holy symbol, monk robes.

COOKING
-------
- Quest start: Cook's Assistant for early levels.
- Progression: shrimps -> sardines/herring -> trout/salmon (fly-caught,
  cook at the Barbarian Village permanent fire) -> tuna -> lobsters ->
  swordfish.
- Cook what you fish. Cook on a range (Lumbridge Castle, Varrock,
  Al-Kharid) for fewer burns.

WOODCUTTING
------------
- Trees -> oaks (15) -> willows (30, Draynor, best XP to 60+) ->
  yews (60, money logs).
- Axes: best you can wield (rune needs 41 Woodcutting). F2P spots:
  Varrock palace, Draynor willows, Edgeville yews, Rimmington yews.
- Drop logs for XP, bank yews for sale.

FLETCHING (members-only training in 274)
-----------------------------------------
- Not trainable in F2P: the dump flags shafts, unstrung bows and
  bowstrings members. F2P can still use basic gear, all flagged F2P:
  shortbow, longbow, oak bows, bronze/iron arrows (see Ranged).

FISHING
-------
- Small net: shrimps (1) + anchovies (15) at Lumbridge/Al Kharid/
  Draynor/Port Sarim. Drop for speed or bank at Al Kharid.
- Bait rod: sardines (5) + herring (10).
- Fly rod + feathers: trout (20) + salmon (30) at Lumbridge/Barbarian
  Village. Drop for speed; cook-then-drop trains Cooking too.
- Harpoon/cage: tuna (35) -> lobsters (40, Karamja) -> swordfish (50,
  Karamja).
- Bait from fishing shops; feathers from chickens or shops.

FIREMAKING
-----------
- Burn logs in order: normal -> oak -> willow (dropped in bulk, the
  classic power method) -> yew.
- Tinderbox + logs anywhere; Varrock/Draynor lines.

CRAFTING
---------
- Quests: Sheep Shearer (wool), Goblin Diplomacy (dye).
- Early: leather (cowhides at the Al-Kharid tannery -> gloves/boots),
  gold jewellery at a furnace (rings, amulets, holy symbols), silver
  items as levels allow.
- Money route: leather and gold rings (see money doc).
- Guild: Crafting Guild (40 Crafting) for silver/gold ores (see money
  doc).

SMITHING
---------
- The Knight's Sword quest: 12,725 XP, 1 -> 29. Do it immediately.
- Rule: always the highest platebody you can make (XP per bar is fixed,
  platebodies use 5 bars). Bronze platebodies 18/29-33, then iron,
  steel, mithril, addy, rune.
- Smelt where the furnace is near a bank (Al-Kharid has furnace+bank
  together); anvil south of Varrock west bank is the closest
  anvil-to-bank in F2P.
- Bars: bronze -> iron -> steel (iron+coal) -> gold -> mithril ->
  addy -> rune (85).

MINING
-------
- Copper+tin (Varrock SE/Lumbridge swamp) -> iron (Al Kharid, Rimmington,
  Dwarven Mine) -> silver (Crafting Guild, Al Kharid) -> coal (Mining
  Guild 60, Falador/Dwarven) -> gold (Crafting Guild) -> mithril ->
  addy -> runite (85, wild 46).
- Pickaxe: best you can wield. Doric's Quest gives
  early Mining XP + access to Doric's anvils.
- Powermine iron for XP (drop ores), bank coal/gold.

RUNECRAFT
----------
- Rune Mysteries quest to start; Aubury in Varrock teleports you to the
  essence mine.
- Altars: air (south Varrock, start here) -> mind -> water -> earth ->
  fire -> body (20). Air/earth stay the F2P staple in 2004. Tiaras and
  talismans grant altar entry.
- Method: mine essence, walk to the altar, craft, bank, repeat. Air
  tiara for direct entry. Runner-assisted crafting via face-to-face
  trade is era-plausible (trade existed, no market needed).

F2P QUESTS WITH SKILL XP (2004, start here before grinding)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Cook's Assistant: 300 Cooking XP (dump).
- Sheep Shearer: 150 Crafting XP (dump).
- Rune Mysteries: unlocks Runecrafting.
- The Knight's Sword: 12,725 Smithing XP (1->29) — verified.
- Doric's Quest: 1,300 Mining XP (dump) + anvil use.
- Goblin Diplomacy: 200 Crafting XP (dump).
- Pirate's Treasure, Prince Ali Rescue, Shield of Arrav, The Restless
  Ghost (Prayer XP), Ernest the Chicken, Vampyre Slayer (4,825 Attack XP,
  dump), Witch's Potion (325 Magic XP, dump): do every F2P quest first; several give
  combat XP that skips the slow early levels.
- XP numbers come from the dump (raw/10); quest F2P status is canonical
  (the dump flags every quest members=None, so it cannot tell);
  LostHQ quest guides target rev 274: https://2004.losthq.rs/
