F2P MONEY MAKING — 2004-2005 ERA (RS2, pre-market)
================================================
Project context: LostCity revision 274 (2004). No market yet (our homemade
exchange, to be implemented later), no generalized bank notes, face-to-face trading mostly
in Varrock / worlds 1-5. Methods only in this doc, by design: no
hourly rates, no prices.

2004 RULES
---------
- Faster respawn in crowded worlds (wines, rocks): guides recommend
  worlds 1-7.
- Fish: only Stiles (south of the Karamja volcano, Musa Point) swaps
  tuna/lobster/swordfish for bank notes. Everything else is banked on
  foot: Karamja ship -> Port Sarim -> Falador/Varrock bank. Count the
  walk into the profit.
- Telegrab magic: without it, the chaos mages attack you.
- Edgeville Dungeon door (hill giants): brass key required.
- F2P runite: a single spot, level 46 Wilderness north of the Lava
  Maze, slow respawn depending on world. Dangerous.
- Rune essence: Rune Mysteries quest required.

METHODS BY LEVEL
----------------

0. STARTER, NO REQUIREMENTS
- Cowhides: Lumbridge cow pen, bank in Al-Kharid or Lumby, stockpile in
  bulk, sell at Al-Kharid bank. Source: Tip.It 2006.
- Feathers: shrimp fishing in Lumbridge, resell feathers to fishers/
  shops.
- Al Kharid iron (2004 account): mine a full load of iron ores, smelt
  bars, smith weapons/armour, sell to the general store. Small profit
  but available at level 1. The witness insists: difficult and tedious.
- Red spider eggs (Varrock sewers): picked up with no skill, resold for
  Herblore use. Aggressive spiders.

1. MERCHANTING (the king 2004-2006, no market)
- Principle: buy low, sell high, worlds 1/3/4/5. Needs starting capital.
- Quoted world rule: W1 low price/demand, W3 medium, W4 high. Buy W1,
  sell W4. Varies with time of day.
- Quoted F2P items: rune trimmed sets and pieces, rune gold sets,
  god armour (zammy and sara full sets, volatile prices), rares (wide
  spreads, fast movers).
- Companies: pay farmers for ores, bars and Zamorak wines, resell
  higher.
- Tip.It + RS forums to sell higher than in game.
- Trap: without price knowledge you lose everything. Scamming = ban,
  mods hang around merchanting worlds.

2. Wines of Zamorak (TELEGRAB) — 33 Magic (37 recommended)
- Chaos Temple north-west of Falador, near the wild. Air staff + law
  runes (+ water at 37). Crowded worlds 1-7 for fast respawn.
- Loop: telegrab -> full inventory -> Falador teleport -> west bank ->
  back. Minimum weight to keep running.
- Resale on the forums. Also Magic XP.
- Variant: buy wines off farmers, resell higher.

3. BIG BONES + ROOTS
- Hill giants (28, Edgeville Dungeon, brass key), moss giants (42),
  ice giants.
- Stockpile in bulk in the bank, sell on world 1.
- Limpwurt roots (hill giants): sought after for Herblore.

4. MINING
- Coal: under Falador, the classic goal.
- Iron: several replies call it better than coal for XP + profit
  (fast respawn).
- Crafting Guild silver/gold (40 Crafting + rune pick). Heavy
  competition on gold, stick to silver.
- Mithril/addy: hop worlds (slow respawn).
- Runite at 85 Mining: high-value ore, but 1 F2P spot (wild 46),
  slow respawn. World hopping mandatory.
- Rune essence: easy low-level mining after Rune Mysteries, steady
  demand from runecrafters.

5. RUNECRAFTING
- Classic air: altar south of Varrock, air tiara, minimum weight.
  Earnings scale with level (multiple runes per essence at high
  levels).
- World 16 runners: people open your noted essence for a tip. High
  volume and fast XP, increasingly worthwhile at high Runecrafting.
- Air/earth stay the F2P staple in 2004.

6. FISHING
- Lobsters/swordfish at Karamja (Musa Point). Note exchange at Stiles
  (tuna/lobster/swordfish only).
- Era trick: buy cheap off fishers training their level, resell higher.
- The queen F2P method with yews: "lobsters and yews back in 05".
- Tuna for Cooking/levelling, not for sale.

7. WOODCUTTING
- Yews at 60 WC: the F2P reference with lobsters ("40+ fishing, 60+
  woodcutting"). Sell logs to fletchers.
- Willows for XP, not profit.

8. SMITHING
- Steel bars (iron + coal): the profitable F2P smithing method.
- Gold bars: smelt/resell.
- Full addy at 88 only. Otherwise slow XP and barely profitable.

9. COMBAT
- Low level: chaos druids (herbs), cows (hides).
- Mid: hill giants (big bones + limpwurts), moss giants.
- High: lesser demons (82), rare drops. Crowded spots, kill-stealers.

10. MISC CRAFTING
- Leather (cowhides at the Al-Kharid tannery into gloves/boots) and gold
  rings at a furnace. Slow but fully F2P (dump flags flax, bowstrings
  and arrow shafts members, so no bowstring spinning in F2P).

QUALITATIVE SUMMARY (no-market world, no figures)
----------------------------------------------
- No reqs: cows/hides, feathers, iron->general store: pocket money.
- Mid level: big bones, coal/iron, wine telegrab: steady solid income.
- High level/capital: air runners, trickle runite, merchanting with
  capital + price knowledge: the biggest gains.
- Duo quoted everywhere in 05: lobsters + yews.
